# Cadence 1.5

## What's new

- **New design, smoother animations.** A refreshed black-first look with consistent spacing, type and controls everywhere, and calmer, smoother transitions.
- **Faster loading.** Cadence opens straight into your last pages from disk and refreshes them once you're signed in.
- **Cover art and play buttons on every song.** Every song row shows its album art and a play button on hover, in playlists, albums, search and more.
- **Player bar links.** Click the song title to open its album, or any artist name to open that artist.
- **Smarter search with a Top result.** Results are ranked by what you're most likely looking for, with a large Top result to play right away.
- **Recently played plays the exact track.** Clicking a Recently played card plays the song you listened to, not just the playlist or album it came from.
- **Playlists search.** Search your playlists right from the sidebar.
- **Smart Autoplay.** When an album, playlist or your queue ends, Cadence keeps playing similar songs, and the player bar shows "Autoplaying similar songs". Turn it off in Settings > Playback.
- **In-app first-time setup.** A setup screen walks you through creating your Spotify app and pasting your Client ID, with a Copy button for the redirect address. No files or settings to edit by hand.
- **Auto-updates.** Settings > Updates tells you when a new version is out and installs it with one click, now or on the next restart. Your Client ID, sign-in and settings are kept.

## Updating from 1.4

Replace `cadence.exe` (or use Settings > Updates from 1.5 on). You stay signed in and nothing needs approving again.
